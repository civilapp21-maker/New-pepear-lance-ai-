Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we store your email address and basic profile info via Supabase authentication so your practice history and Premium status can sync across devices. Signing in is required to run paper analysis; without it you can still browse already-analysed papers anonymously. Fetching papers from MSBTE by paper code also requires signing in.

Papers you upload stay in your browser

PDF extraction (text parsing and OCR) happens entirely on your device using PDF.js and Tesseract.js. Your uploaded files are never sent to our servers. Only the extracted question text is sent to our AI pipeline for clustering and scoring.

Papers fetched from MSBTE

When you enter an MSBTE paper code, Peparlance retrieves the matching previous-year question papers from MSBTE's public e-content portal through our fetching service and adds them to your upload list. Only the paper code and exam session are used for these requests; we don't send your name or email to MSBTE. To make repeat requests faster, a retrieved paper may be kept in our cloud storage (Supabase) and served to other users who ask for the same paper. Once a fetched paper is in your list, it is analysed on your device like any file you upload. Signing in with Google is required to use this feature.

What we store server-side

  • Copies of question papers retrieved from MSBTE, kept as a cache so repeat requests load faster.
  • Technical request logs from our fetching service (such as paper code and exam session), used to keep the service running.
  • Analysis results (question clusters, topics, scores) so repeat uploads of the same papers load instantly from cache.
  • Your practice history and study plan progress, if you're signed in.
  • Basic usage counters to enforce free-tier limits.

Payments

Premium subscription payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details — Razorpay handles that directly and shares with us only the confirmation needed to activate your plan.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, and Google, Groq, Gemini, and OpenRouter for AI processing of extracted question text. Each is bound by its own data-processing terms. We also send your paper-code requests to MSBTE's public e-content portal, through our fetching service hosted on Render, to retrieve the papers you ask for.

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account data at any time from the Profile section, or by contacting us. Signing out or staying signed out at any point stops future data collection tied to your identity.

Terms of Service

Last updated:

The service

Peparlance is an AI-assisted study tool that analyses MSBTE and university past question papers to highlight repeated and high-importance questions. It is a revision aid, not a guarantee of exam content — always verify against your official syllabus. It also lets you retrieve publicly available MSBTE previous-year question papers by entering a paper code.

Free and Premium plans

Core analysis is free with monthly usage limits. Premium removes those limits and unlocks priority features for a subscription fee, billed and processed through Razorpay.

No refunds. All Premium payments are final. Because Premium unlocks digital usage limits immediately on payment, we do not offer refunds or partial refunds once a payment is successfully processed, except where required by applicable law.

Acceptable use

  • Don't upload copyrighted question papers you don't have rights to share, or content unrelated to exam preparation.
  • Don't attempt to abuse, scrape, or reverse-engineer the analysis pipeline or bypass usage limits.
  • Don't use scripts or bots to bulk-fetch papers or overload the paper-fetching feature.
  • Accounts found misusing the service may be suspended without refund.

Question papers fetched from MSBTE

Peparlance can retrieve previous-year question papers that MSBTE (Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education) makes publicly available on its official portal, when you enter a paper code. Peparlance is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by MSBTE. These papers remain the property of MSBTE or their respective owners and are provided only for your personal study and exam preparation; you may not resell or redistribute them. Availability is not guaranteed: a paper may be missing, incomplete, or out of date, so always confirm against the official MSBTE portal. Fetching papers requires you to sign in with Google.

No guarantee of accuracy

Question repetition scores and AI-generated study plans are estimates based on pattern-matching across uploaded papers. They are provided "as is" without warranty, and Peparlance is not liable for exam outcomes based on this guidance.

Changes to the service

Features, limits, and pricing may change as the product evolves. We'll make reasonable efforts to notify users of material changes affecting active Premium subscriptions.
