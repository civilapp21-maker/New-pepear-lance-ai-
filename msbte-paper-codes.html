FAQs

What is an MSBTE paper code?

The paper code (subject code) is the 5 or 6 digit number that identifies a subject in the MSBTE exam, for example 22516 (Operating Systems, I scheme) or 315319 (Operating System, K scheme). It is printed on your hall ticket and question paper.

What is the difference between 5-digit and 6-digit paper codes?

6-digit codes such as 313301 belong to the K scheme. 5-digit codes such as 22305 belong to the I scheme.
