How the MSBTE MCQ mock test works

  1. Enter subject and chapter. Any MSBTE diploma subject works. Pick 5 to 20 questions and a difficulty.

  2. Attempt the quiz. Choose one option for each question. Skip what you are unsure about.

  3. Check score and explanations. See the right answer for every question and revise the weak chapters.

Why practise MCQs for MSBTE exams

MCQs test concepts quickly and show which chapters need more revision. Take a short test after each chapter, repeat the ones you score low in, and combine it with previous year papers to see what the exam actually asks.

Frequently asked questions

Is the MSBTE MCQ mock test free?

Yes. The mock test is free and needs no login. Choose a subject and chapter, press Generate and start the test.

Can I take a chapter-wise MCQ test for any MSBTE subject?

Yes. Type any subject and chapter name, for example Building Construction and Foundations, and AI creates MCQs on that topic.

How many questions can I generate?

You can generate 5, 10, 15 or 20 questions per test at Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty.

Do I get answers and explanations?

Yes. After you submit, you see your score, the correct option for every question and a short explanation.

Are the AI-generated MCQs always correct?

AI can occasionally make mistakes. Use the explanations for revision and verify important answers with your textbook or MSBTE syllabus.

Can I make an MCQ test from a paper code?

Yes. The full Mock Test inside the Peparlance app accepts an MSBTE paper code, reads that paper and builds MCQs on its subject. It also has a timer, question palette and answer review.
