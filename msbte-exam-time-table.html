How to download your time table

  1. Open the official MSBTE time table page.

  3. Select your branch and semester, then generate the timetable.
  4. Print or save it as PDF and match paper codes with your hall ticket.

FAQs

Where can I download the MSBTE exam time table?

On the official MSBTE site open the Examination tab, choose Time Table, select your branch and semester and generate the timetable. You can print it as a PDF.

How many days does the MSBTE theory exam last?

MSBTE theory sessions have run for about 18 days, for example Winter 2025 ran from 11 to 29 November 2025. Check the official timetable for your session.

How do I know my paper code?

The paper code is printed on your hall ticket. Search it on this site to open previous year question papers for that subject.
